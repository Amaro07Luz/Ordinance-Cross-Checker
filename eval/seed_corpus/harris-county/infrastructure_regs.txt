HARRIS COUNTY REGULATIONS FOR INFRASTRUCTURE AND DEVELOPMENT (SYNTHETIC EXCERPT)
ARTICLE 4 ACCESS AND RIGHT-OF-WAY
Section 4.01 Applicability
These regulations apply to all development in unincorporated Harris County that requires access to a county road or right-of-way.
Section 4.02 Driveway Access
A driveway permit is required before constructing any driveway that connects to a county road. Driveways on major thoroughfares shall be spaced a minimum of 150 feet apart, measured from edge to edge.
Section 4.03 Right-of-Way Width
Major thoroughfares shall have a minimum right-of-way width of 100 feet. Local residential streets shall have a minimum right-of-way width of 60 feet.
ARTICLE 6 DRAINAGE
Section 6.01 Stormwater Detention
All new development shall provide on-site stormwater detention sufficient to prevent any increase in peak runoff from the site for the 100-year storm event.
Section 6.02 Floodplain Development
A floodplain development permit is required for any construction within the 100-year floodplain. The lowest finished floor shall be at least 2 feet above the base flood elevation.
