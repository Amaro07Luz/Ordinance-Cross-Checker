Chapter 160 Zoning (SYNTHETIC EXCERPT)

Article IV Residential Districts

§ 160-12. Height regulations.

Maximum building height in residential districts is 35 feet or 2.5 stories, whichever is less. Chimneys, spires and flagpoles are exempt from the height limit.

§ 160-13. Minimum lot area.

The minimum lot area in the R-1 district shall be 20,000 square feet with a minimum lot width of 100 feet.

§ 160-14. Accessory structures.

Accessory structures such as sheds and detached garages shall be set back at least 5 feet from side and rear property lines and shall not exceed 15 feet in height.

Article IX Zoning Permits

§ 160-60. Zoning permit required.

A zoning permit is required before any building permit is issued, any use is changed, or any fence, shed or deck is constructed. Applications are filed with the zoning officer.
