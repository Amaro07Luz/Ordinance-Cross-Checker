CITY OF FULSHEAR UNIFIED DEVELOPMENT CODE (SYNTHETIC EXCERPT)
ARTICLE 3 ZONING DISTRICTS
Section 3.1 Purpose
The zoning districts established in this article promote orderly growth, protect property values and implement the comprehensive plan of the city.
Section 3.2 Residential Setbacks
In the R-1 Single-Family Residential district the minimum front yard setback shall be 25 feet measured from the property line. The minimum side yard setback shall be 7.5 feet and the minimum rear yard setback shall be 20 feet. Corner lots shall provide a 15 foot side yard along the street.
Section 3.3 Building Height
No principal building in a residential district shall exceed 35 feet or two and one-half stories in height, measured from the average finished grade to the highest point of the roof.
Section 3.4 Lot Area and Width
The minimum lot area in the R-1 district shall be 7,200 square feet and the minimum lot width shall be 60 feet at the front building line. Maximum lot coverage shall not exceed 50 percent.
Section 3.5 Off-Street Parking
Each single-family dwelling shall provide at least two off-street parking spaces located behind the front building line or within an enclosed garage.
Section 3.6 Townhouse District
Townhouses are permitted in the TH district only. A townhouse development shall not exceed 12 units per acre and each unit shall have a minimum lot width of 22 feet.
