CITY OF FULSHEAR UNIFIED DEVELOPMENT CODE (SYNTHETIC EXCERPT)
ARTICLE 5 SUBDIVISION REGULATIONS
Section 5.1 Platting Required
A preliminary plat must be submitted to the Planning and Zoning Commission before any subdivision of land within the city limits or its extraterritorial jurisdiction.
Section 5.2 Final Plat
A final plat shall be submitted within two years of preliminary plat approval. Failure to submit a final plat within this period voids the preliminary plat approval.
Section 5.3 Parkland Dedication
Each residential subdivision shall dedicate one acre of parkland for every 100 dwelling units, or pay a fee in lieu of dedication as set by the city fee schedule.
Section 5.4 Sidewalks
Sidewalks of at least five feet in width are required on both sides of all public streets within a subdivision.
