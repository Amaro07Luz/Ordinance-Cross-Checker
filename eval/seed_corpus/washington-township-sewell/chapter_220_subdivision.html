Chapter 220 Land Subdivision (SYNTHETIC EXCERPT)

Article II Application Procedures

§ 220-4. Minor subdivision.

A minor subdivision is a subdivision of land into not more than three lots that does not require new streets. Minor subdivision approval requires submission of a plat, a completed application and the application fee to the Planning Board.

§ 220-5. Major subdivision.

Any subdivision that is not a minor subdivision is a major subdivision and requires preliminary and final approval by the Planning Board, including a public hearing with notice to property owners within 200 feet.

Article IV Design Standards

§ 220-21. Lots.

Lot dimensions and area shall not be less than the requirements of Chapter 199 Site Plan Review and the zoning ordinance. Side lot lines shall be at right angles to straight street lines.
