MONTGOMERY COUNTY ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH PERMITTING (SYNTHETIC EXCERPT)
Section 2.1 On-Site Sewage Facilities
A permit from the Environmental Health Services Department is required before installing, altering or repairing any on-site sewage facility. The minimum lot size for a lot served by an on-site sewage facility and a private water well is one acre.
Section 2.2 Subdivision Review
Subdivision plats in unincorporated Montgomery County shall be reviewed by Environmental Health for sewage disposal suitability before approval by Commissioners Court.
Section 2.3 Permit Fees
The fee for an on-site sewage facility permit for a single-family residence is set by the county fee schedule and is due when the application is submitted.
